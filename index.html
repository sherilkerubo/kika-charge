ℹ️ P2P Charging Architecture

🔌 Decentralized Solar Sharing

Instead of building multi-million dollar charging depots on weak urban grid systems, Kika-Charge harnesses the massive scale of private solar installations. Kiosk owners, market vendors, and homes with rooftop solar generate dynamic surplus electricity during daylight peak hours. By selling this excess directly to boda-boda operators, they earn solar passive income and relieve the urban grid.

📡 How SMS / Map-Free Routing Works

Maps consume excessive mobile data and battery life, and they fail in rural areas with poor 3G/4G networks. Kika-Charge works by:

  1. Local SQLite Cache: Core database of kiosk names, directions, and geographic zones is stored locally on the phone.
  2. Delimiter-based Pings: Real-time requests (checking battery inventory and prices) are serialized into tiny packets.
  3. Multi-Channel Broadcast: Packets can be sent over standard cellular internet (via custom UDP/TCP headers) or converted to a simple SMS string when internet drops completely.

☀️ Sun-Based Dynamic Pricing

To prevent grid spikes and optimize clean energy consumption, charging fees dynamically scale with solar generation:

  • Midday Peak (11 AM - 3 PM): Solar energy is abundant. Prices are reduced by up to 30% to incentivize battery swapping during peak sun.
  • Overcast / Night: Grid-fallback mode. Prices return to baseline rates to preserve grid stability.
